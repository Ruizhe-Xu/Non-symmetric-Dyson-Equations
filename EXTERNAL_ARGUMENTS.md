# Explicit external mathematical arguments

This formalization treats the **general mathematical principles** listed below as explicit `Prop` parameters, as permitted for the source development. They are supplied to the paper-facing theorems or reusable backend results. Lean checks the implications **conditional on those parameters**. The main mathematical conclusions specific to the Dyson equation—such as density classification, nondegeneracy, stability estimates, and selection of continuous physical branches—have not simply been postulated as external assumptions.

A global `#print axioms` check reports axioms used by proof terms, but **does not list ordinary hypotheses in a theorem's type**. Thus absence of `sorryAx` or project-added global axioms is not enough to establish an unconditional proof. Inspect the actual theorem declarations; the verification script can generate `validation/theorem_signatures.log` and the full axiom audit.

The tree-level Schur reduction, ℓ² operator construction, and coordinate restrictions are implemented in the project rather than taken as external model-specific inputs. The Poisson density profiles used in M6 derive from the actual M5 asymptotics and measure inversion; the model-level M6 statements do not assume the required inverse bounds or density scales. General spectral-perturbation interfaces encapsulate finite-dimensional Rouché/analytic perturbation conclusions, but the Dyson-specific Schur identities, coefficients, normalization, and boundary interpretation are checked internally.

Proof-data structures such as `LocalReduction`, `PhysicalNormalForm`, `FrontierData`, and `SimpleSpectralSplit` carry constructions developed along the proof route. They should not be confused with assumptions that directly state the final Dyson conclusions.

The supported global foundational axioms are `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. The verification script checks for forbidden proof placeholders and global project axioms and audits transitive axiom dependencies of named theorems. **These checks do not certify the truth of the explicit external mathematical arguments listed above.**
